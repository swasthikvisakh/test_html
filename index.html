Swasthik Visakh S

Junior Research Fellow Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST)

I study how gas moves and clumps inside our Galaxy. My thesis follows the kinematics and hierarchical structure of the Galactic cloud G316.80-0.05, and my other projects cover X-ray data from XPoSat and exoplanet detection. I also photograph the night sky and take part in public astronomy outreach in Kerala.
