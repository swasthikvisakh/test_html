Swasthik Visakh S

Junior Research Fellow & Ph.D. Scholar Department of Earth and Space Sciences, Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST)

My research is in high-energy astrophysics, focusing on the broadband spectral and timing properties of accreting compact objects, neutron star X-ray binaries, and X-ray pulsars using space-based observatories. Alongside observational X-ray astronomy, I have worked on molecular cloud kinematics, exoplanet detection, astrophotography, and science outreach across Kerala.

Research

Current Doctoral Research

Spectro-temporal studies of accreting neutron star X-ray binaries and X-ray binary pulsars

Investigating the accretion dynamics, broadband X-ray spectral continuum, cyclotron resonance features, spin evolution, and pulse profile variations of accreting X-ray pulsars during outbursts using observations from space-based X-ray missions.
